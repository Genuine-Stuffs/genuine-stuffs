Design a 4-storey, 3-star boutique hotel of 34 rooms on a 30 m × 40 m plot in Lagos outside the traditional core, to the Lagos Physical Planning Permit Regulations 2019 and the National Building Code 2006. Ground floor: reception and lobby, lounge/bar, a 40-seat restaurant (about one seat per room) with its kitchen, public WCs, an accessible guest room, admin office, staff facilities and a generator room. Three guest floors, each with 10 standard rooms of 24 m² and one suite of 40 m² (the first floor's suite accessible), all en-suite, on a 1.8 m double-loaded corridor with no dead end over 6 m and no room more than 30 m from a stair. Two escape stairs at least 1.2 m wide, one at each end of the corridor, a guest lift, and a service lift beside a housekeeping room on every floor that does not open onto the guest corridor. Setbacks 9 m front and 4.5 m sides and rear, building within 70% of the plot with 10% landscaping, and 14 parking bays (1 per 3 rooms plus 1 per 40 m² of restaurant, 1 accessible). Provide a ground-floor plan, a typical guest-floor plan, and a 3D exterior render.
