Design a single-storey church auditorium seating 1,000 people on a 60 m × 80 m standard plot in Lagos, to the Lagos Physical Planning Permit Regulations 2019 and the National Building Code 2006. Main hall about 34 m wide and 21 m deep for 1,000 seats in straight rows (0.95 m row pitch, at most 14 seats between aisles, a 1.5 m centre aisle and 1.1 m side aisles), a clear-span steel portal roof, and the hall no higher than 7.5 m. An altar/stage about 8 m deep with a choir and instrument area for about 100 singers and an instrument store beside it; a pastor's vestry off the stage leading to the pastor's office and an administration office. An entrance foyer of about 200 m² with the main entrance at least 3 m wide, and four exits in all for an occupant load of about 1,170. An overflow / children's church for about 100 children, a nursing mothers' room beside it, an ushers' room with a counting room, a bookshop, and a sound and media booth at the back of the hall. Restrooms off the foyer: 17 WCs for women, 6 WCs and 8 urinals for men, basins, and an accessible WC; 10 wheelchair spaces in the hall. Keep the building within 50% of the plot with 10% landscaping, setbacks 9 m front and 4.5 m sides and rear, and parking for 95 cars (1 per 10 m² of worship floor, 5 accessible). Provide a dimensioned floor plan with the seating layout and a 3D exterior render.
