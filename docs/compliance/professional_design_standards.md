# Material Insight: Professional Design Standards & Compliance

Welcome to the **Material Insight AI Studio**. Our design engine is engineered specifically for the Nigerian construction landscape, ensuring that your dream project is built on a foundation of legal and technical excellence.

## 🛡️ Built-in Compliance Assurance
Every plan, model, and specification generated by the AI Studio is automatically cross-referenced against the **Nigerian National Building Code (NBC) 2006** and local zoning laws (LASPPPA/FCDA).

### 📐 Structural Fidelity
The AI Studio does not just "draw" buildings; it calculates them. 
*   **Beam & Slab Verification:** All structural suggestions adhere to mandatory span-to-depth ratios and reinforcement spacing.
*   **Load Bearing:** Designs are weighted against standard residential and commercial load assumptions (kN/m2) to ensure stability.

### 🏡 Spatial Standards
We guarantee that every habitable space meets the minimum health and comfort requirements set by the Federation:
*   **Living Areas:** Min 12.0m² for ventilation and comfort.
*   **Master Bedrooms:** Min 9.0m² (Optimized to 12-16m² for premium designs).
*   **Kitchens & Services:** Functional layouts with mandatory minimum widths.

### 🚩 Legal Setback Management
Never worry about illegal encroachment. The AI Studio automatically applies regional setback requirements (e.g., 6m front, 3m side for Lagos Residential) based on your site data.

## 📄 Professional Artifact Package
A design is only as good as its documentation. The AI Studio provides a complete, professional bundle for your contractors and regulatory bodies:
*   **Architectural Packet:** Site plans, floor plans, and elevations.
*   **Priced BOQ:** Accurate material list based on current mix ratios and market prices.
*   **Safety & Quality Plans:** Mandatory Health & Safety (H&S) and Quality Management (QMP) documents required for legal site operation.
*   **Buildability Report:** An analysis ensuring your design can be realistically built by local contractors.

## 🤝 Professional Registry
While our AI is powerful, it is designed to work *with* professionals. Our outputs are structured to be signed off by registered Architects, Engineers, and Builders, facilitating a smooth path to your **Certificate of Fitness for Habitation**.
